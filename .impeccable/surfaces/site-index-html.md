# Surface brief: paper theme (Work, About, Imprint)

Scope: the third theme "paper" across `site/index.html`, `site/about.html`, `site/imprint.html` (shared tokens in `site/paper.js`). Mode: Experience (portfolio; the work leads, the interface recedes). Audience: tech recruiters and engineering teams. Constraints: layout, content and behaviour stay as in light/dark; only the material changes. Body text must be readable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Elegant-modern stationery, after the Maison Noyer identity (croire.bureau): uncoated greige paper, soft colour fields and fine print. Refuses the cream-scrapbook and the cobalt office-supply spikes before it.

OWN-WORLD: Ground greige #ECE8E1 with paper grain; sheets #FAF8F4 with thin wine crop marks in the corners. Colours: wine #6C143B (headlines, active states, buttons with pink #FFCCDA text), olive #4C6D35 field with white text, lime #BFD485, pink #FFCCDA, and the olive→lime→pink gradient as the signature field. Pinyon Script (the hello font of light and dark) for large headlines; Tenor Sans for smaller titles (tabs, project label, section heads); Inter for all text. The Work page carries a full-strength horizon after the Maison Noyer prints: moss olive, a wide walnut field and mist blue, then back into greige at the bottom, with eased (smoothstep) stops so no bands show; the card wheel is up to 20% larger (as far as the viewport height allows) and its side cards stay fully opaque. A silver paperclip and milky vellum chips over the portrait. Rounded frames (14–22px), pill controls.

STORY: The visitor reads a calm, well-made printed portfolio: projects, profile, stack, CV and contact without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Work: olive-topped horizon, light wordmark, paper pill nav with wine active state, project cards framed in paper white, the current title in a paper pill below the wheel, arranged as in light and dark (year and category small above, Pinyon Script title below).

FORM: pinned brief (reference site and four colours given by the user). seed: none (pinned) — "elegant-modern mit paper elementen und texturen … 6C143B, 4C6D35, BFD485, FFCCDA … hintergrund weiß-beige-greige".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
